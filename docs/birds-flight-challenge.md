## Implementation and review

Wing recovery and downstroke differ smoothly. Heading derives from analytic patrol velocity and turns continuously through a frontal pose; banking and pitch follow the flight path. Cached `Path2D` feather geometry bounds per-frame work. Decorative motion quiets under reduced-motion settings; essential target patrols remain usable. Birds freeze/resume with the existing host lifecycle. The torso stays centered on the unchanged collision target.

The first six levels retain their names and bird counts but now have offset approaches, steel shelves, separated two-bird lanes, wider/faster predictable patrols, and ink budgets of 200/190/175/210/200/270. Split Flight uses two drawn turns. Hints were updated to match workable routes. Three-bird layouts, rewards, saved stage indices, and storage version remain unchanged.

Actual desktop and phone frames at several wing phases show coherent body volume, silhouettes, natural palettes, and feather overlap without clipping or new page overflow.
