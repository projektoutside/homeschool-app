Explicit request: make platforms and player-drawn lines more realistic and consistent with the new dropping mechanism, and let the ball reach the floor of the scene where possible. Observed causes: pocket-shaped colliders are hidden by the renderer, and a win freezes physics and opens the result immediately. ## Result and evidence

Steel platform faces now have bevels, brass end fittings, fasteners, wear, and cast shadows. Player paths use continuous brass tubing with highlights, collars, and end fasteners, including the live drawing preview. Platform and committed-path artwork is cached; ink measurement, drawing bounds, and collision widths are retained. The invisible catch-basin colliders were removed; their legacy level metadata still positions the birds and hints. The ground is at world y=626, aligned with the forest foreground, and the sphere lands at approximately y=614 with radius 12.

Clearing the last bird still awards/unlocks once immediately, while the result waits for the stone to land or settle on an actual visible support. Solid runways continue to support the stone; they do not become pass-through geometry. Retry cancels a pending result without a timer. Flight timeout now starts at release rather than consuming the player's drawing/planning time. Short supported rests are detected above the former arbitrary y=220 cutoff as well. First landing uses the existing particle language and bounce cue, plus a contact shadow; resting contact does not continually retrigger the impact ring.

Two bounded passes in the same visual direction: initial review exposed a contact-state gap at the real floor; the targeted revision uses a 0.15-world-pixel contact tolerance so fractional fixed steps recognize support consistently.
