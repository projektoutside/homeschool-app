The first mission introduces the original three skills, then the three new skills. Subsequent decks shuffle all six, avoid immediate repeated families and discourage recent duplicate puzzles. Unfinished decks carry into replay so a victory does not discard unexplored activities. Pattern progression covers AB, AAB, ABB, ABC and AABB, retaining a visible complete teaching unit before the missing item.
