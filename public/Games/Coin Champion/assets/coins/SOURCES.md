identify these images as public domain. Original photographs remain unmodified;
the browser reuses the game's edge-connected matte cleanup to prepare textures.

are credited below. No Mint or government sponsorship or endorsement is implied.

These are familiar denomination designs from different years, not a matched
numismatic specimen or a claim about the newest annual designs. Specifications
for diameter, thickness, and edge treatment follow the
[United States Mint coin specification table](https://www.usmint.gov/learn/coins-and-medals/circulating-coins/coin-specifications).
The [Mint design-use policy](https://www.usmint.gov/news/image-library/circulating-coin-design-use-policy)
and each file's metadata provide the source context. `sources.json` records the
download references and file credits.

The renderer reuses the repository's Three.js r160 file at
`public/Games/analogclockgame/three.min.js` under its existing MIT license header.
