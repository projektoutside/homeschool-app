How to Play

  1. Build: choose a defender, then choose an open stone pad.
  2. Upgrade: select a placed defender to strengthen its skills.
  3. Sell: reclaim coins from a selected defender and change your plan.
  4. Protect hearts: each enemy that reaches the castle costs one heart.
  5. Pause: use the pause control whenever you need time to think.
  6. Speed: switch between 1× and 2× once your defense is ready.
