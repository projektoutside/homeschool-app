Field guide

How to Play

  1. Build by role: put Road melee defenders on road squares and Grass ranged defenders on grass squares.
  2. Watch frontline health: enemies wind up, strike, and reduce the health bars beneath Road melee defenders.
  3. Replace permanent defeats: a Road melee defender at zero health is removed, and its road square becomes available for a fresh purchase.
  4. Upgrade: select a placed defender to strengthen its exact role.
  5. Sell: reclaim 70% of invested coins, but not while a Road melee defender is fighting.
  6. Protect hearts: enemies that pass every road guard damage the castle.
  7. Read warnings: boss telegraphs are strategic information, not decoration.
  8. Control time: pause freely or use 2× speed after your formation is ready.
