La’s Homeschool Hub Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

This policy describes how Xatori handles information in the Android edition of La’s Homeschool Hub distributed through Google Play. For privacy or support questions, email xatori@xatoridev.com.

Guest play and saved progress

The Android app can be used as a guest. Cloud account features are disabled in the Google Play build. The app stores your guest identifier, preferences, and progress, such as points, stars, stamina, and game saves, in its local app storage so you can resume play. Android may include app data in device backups according to your device settings.

Voice answers

Car King and Animal Champion offer optional voice answers. If you choose to use voice and grant microphone permission, the app sends microphone input to the Android speech recognition service selected by your device. That provider may process audio on its servers. The app uses the returned text to check your answer. Xatori does not receive audio recordings through a Homeschool Hub server or save the microphone audio for playback.

You can choose touch controls and revoke microphone permission in Android Settings. The speech recognition provider’s privacy and retention practices apply to its processing.

Narration and other online resources

Spy Academy can send narration text to StreamElements to generate audio, including a player name you enter if it appears in a spoken line. You can use a nickname for play. The game can also use the device’s speech synthesis service. If you configure a third-party speech provider in the game, the app uses the credentials you supply to request narration directly from that provider. The provider’s privacy and retention practices apply to those requests.

Google Play handles app installation and the optional learning library download. Some activities load fonts, software libraries, or interactive resources from other providers, such as Google Fonts, Cloudflare’s cdnjs, and NASA. Those services can receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address when the app requests a resource. Linked resources are governed by the provider’s own privacy policy.

The app does not display advertising or sell earned game points.

Children and families

The app includes activities for children. A parent or guardian can choose whether to allow microphone access and help children use nicknames in shared games. Contact us if you have questions about a child’s information or would like to request its deletion.

Security, retention, and deletion

Android’s app storage separates saved progress and settings from other apps. Online services described in this policy receive requests over encrypted connections. Local information stays on the device until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. Backups are managed through Android settings and may be restored separately.

If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond and handle your request. Our email provider processes that correspondence, and we retain it as needed to resolve the matter or meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion by emailing xatori@xatoridev.com.
