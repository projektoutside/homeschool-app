## Product Purpose

Observed: the homeschool hub brings browser learning games, worksheets, tools, and classroom experiences together. Games also run in an embedded player and a Capacitor shell.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Observed: Many Birds One Stone is a portrait physics puzzle: draw paths within an ink budget, position a claw, and release one stone to clear moving birds. Preserve its levels, physics, saved progress, points contract, host lifecycle, and standalone launch.

Explicit task: improve simultaneous VS handwriting, microphone answer recognition and visible correct-answer feedback, and Many Birds One Stone visuals and ElevenLabs sound effects. Physical device reliability remains a verification gate.

## Users

Inferred from the app and requested games: learners playing alone or together on shared touch screens. Specific ages and accessibility needs are not established.
